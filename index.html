    A LITTLE FIELD GUIDE

    Look. Remember. Hop.

    1. Memorize the landmines.Choose a difficulty, then start. Study the marked tiles before the countdown ends.
    2. Find the finish from memory.All mines disappear. The rabbit appears at the start and the flag marks the finish. Move up, down, left, or right; diagonal moves do not count.
    3. You decide your final route.Tap adjacent tiles, drag, or use arrow keys / WASD. Step backward or use Undo to change your route. The mines stay hidden, even if you cross one.
    4. The finish reveals everything.Reaching the flag checks your final path. No mines: success. Any mine: try a fresh field. Every random field has a safe solution.
    5. Play 10 rounds and build your score.Start at 6×6. A win moves you up one level; a loss stays at the same level. Two consecutive losses at one level move you down. Only wins score points. Complete all 10 rounds to save your daily best.

    There is no time limit while finding your path. If you leave the tab during the preview, a fresh round will be prepared.
